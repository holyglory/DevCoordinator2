# User Issue Ledger: Security

| ID | Applies to | Mistake pattern | Required behavior | Prevention and verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UIL-SECURITY-001 | Decisions that add, change, weaken, remove, or intentionally omit security-posture controls | Agents applied generic maximum-hardening measures without establishing material operating assumptions, or demanded a full threat-model interview when invoking one reviewed skill or tool without changing its documented security posture. | Base every security-posture decision on project-specific, user-confirmed assumptions recorded in project-root `security-assumptions.md`. Ask only when a concrete pending control decision depends on an unresolved assumption and a wrong answer could select unnecessary controls, omit a necessary control, expand the work, or cause meaningful rework. Ask the smallest concise set of material questions and make detail proportional to impact; require the full baseline only when that decision materially depends on every area. A routine reviewed invocation that preserves documented controls uses the existing record and does not reopen it. Record unresolved material assumptions explicitly; they cannot justify a control. Never invent assumptions or default to maximum hardening. | Require the security-assumption gate, decision-material threshold, proportional-question contract, and routine-invocation false-positive guard in universal policy; add must-catch fixtures for unconfirmed material assumptions, inferred threats, unconditional hardening, and weakened/removed/omitted controls plus false-positive guards for non-security work, read-only discovery, established-posture skill use, and assumption-backed proportional controls; verify each implemented security-posture decision cites its supporting assumptions. |
